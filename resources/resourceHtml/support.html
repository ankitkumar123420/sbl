FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Browse our frequently asked questions or search for something specific. Click any question to see its answer.

What is SBL? +

SBL is a software development and business technology company focused on building digital solutions that help businesses manage operations, workflows, data, automation and business processes.

What type of software does SBL develop? +

SBL develops business-focused software including management platforms, ERP solutions, CRM systems, logistics software, workflow automation, AI-powered solutions and customized business applications.

Can SBL develop custom software for my business? +

Yes. SBL can develop software according to specific business requirements, workflows, integrations, automation needs and operational processes.

Which businesses can use SBL solutions? +

SBL solutions are designed around business operations and can be adapted to different organizations, teams and workflows. The appropriate solution depends on the requirements of the business.

Can I contact SBL before purchasing a solution? +

Yes. You can contact SBL before choosing a solution to discuss your business requirements, software needs, integrations, customization requirements and availability.

How do I create an SBL account? +

Account creation depends on the particular SBL software you are using. Follow the registration process provided by the product or contact SBL support if you need assistance.

I forgot my password. What should I do? +

Use the password recovery option available on the product's login page. If you cannot recover your account, contact SBL support for assistance.

Can I change my account information? +

Available account settings depend on the particular SBL product and your permissions. Check your account settings or contact support if you cannot change the required information.

Can multiple employees use SBL software? +

Multi-user access depends on the specific product and its user-management capabilities. Where supported, users can be configured according to their roles and responsibilities.

What SBL products are available? +

SBL is building a connected ecosystem of business software including ERP, CRM, Logistics, SBL Flow, AI and automation solutions and specialized business platforms.

Are all SBL products currently available? +

No. Some SBL products and modules are still under development. Product pages indicate whether a solution is currently available or coming soon.

Can SBL products work together? +

SBL's ecosystem is designed around connected business operations. Integration between products depends on the particular products, implementation and available integration capabilities.

Can I request a new feature? +

Yes. You can contact the SBL team with a feature request. Describe what you want to achieve and how the feature would improve your business workflow.

The software is not loading. What should I do? +

First check your internet connection and refresh the page. You can also try an updated browser. If the issue continues, contact support and provide the affected page and details of the problem.

Why is the software running slowly? +

Performance can be affected by network conditions, browser issues, large data operations or application issues. Check your connection and refresh the application first. If the problem continues, contact support with details about the issue.

What information should I provide when reporting an issue? +

Include the product name, affected page, description of the problem, steps that caused it, approximate time, error messages and screenshots when available.

Can SBL integrate with existing business software? +

Integration depends on the systems involved and their available technical interfaces. SBL can evaluate requirements such as APIs, data exchange, authentication and workflow connections.

Does SBL provide software updates? +

SBL software can receive improvements, fixes and new capabilities as products evolve. Update processes depend on the particular product and deployment environment.

How does SBL pricing work? +

Pricing can vary according to the software, features, users, deployment requirements and business requirements. Contact SBL for pricing information for the solution you need.

Can I request a custom software quotation? +

Yes. Share your requirements, workflows, integrations and expected project scope with SBL so the appropriate solution and quotation can be discussed.

Does customization affect the project cost? +

Customization requirements can affect project scope and pricing. Applicable development and implementation costs should be discussed with SBL before work begins.

Can software requirements be changed later? +

Requirement changes can be discussed during development or implementation. Their impact depends on the scope and development stage.

How does SBL handle business data? +

Data handling depends on the specific product, deployment model and technical architecture. For product-specific information, contact the SBL team.

Who can access information inside the software? +

Access depends on the product's user and permission configuration. Where role-based access is supported, administrators can manage access according to user responsibilities.

What should I do if I notice suspicious account activity? +

Contact SBL support as soon as possible and provide relevant details. If available, secure your account by changing your password and reviewing account access.

Can I request information about data protection? +

Yes. Contact SBL if you need product-specific information about data handling, access controls, storage or other security requirements.

How can I contact SBL support? +

You can contact the SBL team through the Contact Us page. For technical issues, include the product, affected page, error message and screenshots when available.

What information should I provide when contacting support? +

Tell us which product you are using, describe the problem, explain what you were trying to do, mention any error message and attach screenshots when useful.

Can I discuss my business requirements with SBL? +

Yes. You can contact SBL to discuss your business processes, software requirements, automation needs, integrations and potential custom solutions.
